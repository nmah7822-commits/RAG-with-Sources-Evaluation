# Bloom & Co Store FAQs (sample data)

## Shipping
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Shipping is free on orders over $50, otherwise it costs $4.99.
Express delivery takes 1-2 business days and costs $12.99.
We also ship internationally to 20 countries. International delivery takes 7-14 business days.

## Returns and refunds
You can return an item within 14 days of delivery if it is unworn and the tags are attached.
Refunds take 5-7 business days and go back to the original payment method.
The first exchange for any order is free.

## Orders and payment
You can cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it. After that, the order is already being packed.
We accept credit cards, debit cards and PayPal. Cash on delivery is available for orders under $200.

## Support and gift cards
Customer support is available Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm. Email us at support@bloomco.example.
Gift cards are valid for 12 months from the purchase date.
